# Multi-AI review meta-analysis — 2026-11-02

## Summary
- Verdict: **Fix first, then continue.** Coin conservation holds and the architecture is sound, but the
  shipped game does not run at the speed on record, three state defects are reproduced, and the long-run
  economy problem traces to a rule that was never carried through the code.
- **Speed.** With the shipped `config.csv` on the full-scale map a turn takes about **8.6 s** in the Editor
  (39 turns measured), about 30 times the 290 ms the scale test reports. That test runs on code defaults and misses this.
  Merchant bids with three-hop judging cost 8.4 s of it; `agents.credit` costs another 0.8 s, and no reviewer
  reported that.
- **State defects reproduced.** Defense purchases remove goods twice (C01). A facility held by the bank goes
  bankrupt with its auction open (C03). Loading turns a facility cash of exactly -1 into start cash, which
  conjures coins (C02), though that value never occurred in 145,877 facility-weeks.
- **Economy.** The 25% trader margin (DEC-098) stops at the sale: 7 of 13 processing recipes lose money at
  anchor (C20) while investment, working cash and bids still value output at list price (C21). A documented
  necessity elasticity of 0.5 acts as about 0.97 (C19), so a 15% price rise reads as 85.5% supply.
- **What holds.** The ledger equalled the coin supply on both continents in all eight sample years measured.
  Assembly direction is acyclic. Reviewers traced the normal money paths and found them balanced.
- **This review is not complete.** Six of ten planned reviews arrived, from two of four vendors. Only 2 of 8
  known unfixed issues were found again, and the estimates below put 30% or more of the defects still unfound.
- Scale: 77 findings became 59 distinct issues: 8 high, 22 medium, 29 low. 54 are confirmed by running, reading or
  redoing the arithmetic; 5 are unverified. None was refuted. Nothing was fixed.

Findings:
- **Lenses found different things; vendors rarely agreed outside L3.** Five of the eight shared issues are in L3,
  where the brief named specific hot paths and tests. Shared issues are mostly easy to see from the code; 7 of the
  8 high issues came from one vendor only.
- **Vendors differ in depth, not accuracy.** All 13 Codex issues were confirmed, and 49 of 54 Claude issues
  (the other 5 unverified). Claude found about four times as many, at a far higher cost in tokens: its workers
  contributed to hitting the shared usage limit during this review. No high issue depended on Codex.
- **A reviewer's severity depends on its lens.** C04 was high, low and medium from three reviewers. C17 was low
  in L1 and high in L2. Measuring settled these: C04 is 0.5 to 0.8%, so low. Reviewers' confidence figures did
  not help: Codex averaged 95 to 98, Claude 82 to 85, on similar accuracy.
- **The one real disagreement is on population.** L2-claude calls long-run decline a high defect of the clamp and
  migration; L2-codex lists the same arithmetic as a strength, since the formula does what the rule says. Both
  are right: the code honours the rule, and the rule produces decline (C18).

## Limits of this analysis
- Figures are from the Editor with no player build. Attribution uses the first 8 turns. The new-year figure uses
  cut-down merchant settings, because the shipped settings did not reach turn 52 in the time allowed; it excludes
  merchant cost. The run to turn 39 logged as failed on NUnit's 180 s limit after its figures were recorded.
- C01 ran the price step on the state the job leaves, not the job. C03 and C02 set their starting state by hand.
  C20 is a static sum at anchor prices, not a running economy; prices do move away from anchor in play.
- The meta-reviewer is the same model family as the Claude reviewers, and most confirmations rest on its own
  reading, so they are not fully independent. Vendor comparisons above carry that bias.
- Reviewers shared one brief, so the capture-recapture assumption of independence does not hold. Severity scales
  differ between vendors. L3-claude saw two file names in `Docs/GDD/reviews/` in a folder listing without opening them.
- Five issues remain unverified: C24, C25, C37, C55, C57. Magnitudes in C18, C22 and C30 are arithmetic, not runs.
